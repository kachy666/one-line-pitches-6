> You already pay $14 a month for a meal-planning app that has never once told you what's for dinner, so this one texts you the recipe at 5pm and the shopping list at noon.

The pitch's angle is blunt: you're already paying — $14 a month for a meal-planning app that has never once told you what's for dinner. The twist is the paid app fails at its one job, so the new one earns its keep instead: the recipe texted at 5pm, the shopping list at noon, each arriving exactly when you need it.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the pitch (one sentence, at most 240 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
